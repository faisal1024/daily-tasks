## 1.5 B: Plus moments

- **Evening teaser.** A free user who closes a perfect day (every task done, at least
  `EVENING_TEASER_MIN_DONE` = 3, in `lib/daily-tasks/evening-teaser.ts`) sees one real
  line and two placeholder bars, "With Plus, your coach drafts tomorrow's three tonight."
  and **See all three with Plus** (paywall source `evening_draft`). No AI call for free
  users. The line comes from the device: a task still open, else the oldest one saved for
  later, else the built-in idea for the next step on the goal path; with none, the old
  "Tomorrow starts fresh." stays. Buying from it closes the day again with the AI coach.
  The check-in card, the done card and the "Tonight" line only promise a coach's draft
  when the AI coach will close the day (Plus with the proxy).
- **Paywall.** Benefits lead with Nag mode, "Your coach drafts tomorrow", Smart brain dump,
  Break it down. Lifetime shows on every paywall once this install has closed two without
  buying (`LIFETIME_AFTER_DISMISSALS`, key `daily-tasks/plus-paywall-dismissals`; event
  `paywall_dismissed` with `source` and `count`). Settings › Plus still always shows it.
- **One free Break it down.** Client: `FREE_BREAK_DOWNS` = 1 in `free-uses.ts` (claimed
  before the call, given back if the AI can't be reached, set to used on a 402), "1 free
  try" next to the button. Server: the break-down route allows a free id
  `FREE_BREAK_DOWNS_PER_CLIENT` (1) try ever, in the Limits object's new `once_counts`
  table, within `FREE_BREAK_DOWNS_PER_NETWORK_PER_DAY` (20) free tries per network a day;
  a failed AI call gives the try back. A reinstall gets a new RevenueCat anonymous id and
  so one more try; the network and global caps bound that.
- **Ship order:** deploy the Worker (`cd server && npx wrangler@4 deploy`) before the 1.5
  build goes out. With the old Worker the free try gets a 402 and the app shows the
  paywall, so nothing breaks, but the try doesn't work.
