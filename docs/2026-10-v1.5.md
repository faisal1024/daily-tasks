# 1.5 — release notes for the team

Each 1.5 PR appends its own section below.

## 1.5 C: reasons under picks, smarter memory, "What your coach remembers"

**Reasons under picks.** The brain-dump route now returns a short reason per
pick (`reason`, at most five words and 40 characters, e.g. "Quick win",
"Moved 3 times"); the brain-dump review and first run's "Here are your three"
show it muted under each pick. Too long, a link, the task's own words, or a
move count the app didn't send: dropped (server and app). No reason (an older
Worker): the app uses "Moved N times" from its own memory, and the on-device
split says "First on your list" / "Next on your list". Not shown on the Today
card (the reason isn't stored with the task).

**Moved count.** Derived from history, no new task field: a past day a task
(same words, ignoring case and spacing) was on and not finished, after which
it came back (carried over, or on a later day's list in the window, today
included). Routine tasks don't count. "Not today" leaves no trace in history,
so it isn't counted.

**Basic memory (everyone).** `lib/daily-tasks/coach-memory.ts`, from the last
30 days before today: tasks finished of set, best weekdays (seen twice or
more, better than the overall rate and at least half done; up to two), tasks
moved twice or more (up to three), and kinds of task by first word (three or
more tasks: 80%+ done "usually get done", a third or less "tend to slip").
Nothing is stored; it is worked out each time.

**Sent to the AI.** `stats: { days, done, total, bestDays?, moved? }` with
brain dumps (free and Plus) and the plan request (next to the five-day
summary, which is unchanged). Nag lines get `moved` per task. A malformed
`stats` is ignored by the Worker, never a 400. Nothing new goes to analytics.
The privacy policy says so (the published gist copy needs the same update).

**What your coach remembers.** A Settings section for everyone: the basic
memory in plain sentences; for Plus, the AI coach's notes (`coachMemory`),
which can be edited (Save/Cancel, 500 characters). "Clear memory" asks first,
then empties the notes and sets `memoryClearedAt` to today: the basic memory,
the stats sent and the nag weights look only at the days after it. The
history itself (calendar, streaks) is untouched.

**Nag mode leans on dodged tasks.** Built-in lines share their named lines by
weight 1 + times moved (at most 3), spread out by a smooth weighted round
robin; equal weights give exactly the old rotation. Times, counts and every
other rule are unchanged. The AI is told the move counts and asked to give
those tasks more lines.

**Deploy order.** Deploy the Worker before the app ships, or picks show no AI reasons (an older
Worker works with the new app: no reasons, `stats` ignored; the new Worker
works with older apps: the extra `reason` is ignored).
