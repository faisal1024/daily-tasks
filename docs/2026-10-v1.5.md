# 1.5 (October 2026)

Each 1.5 PR adds its own section below.

## 1.5 A: first run and locking

**First run is three screens:** brain dump → "Here are your three" → Today.

- The "Your three are set." nudge screen, the widget how-to screen and the
  end-of-first-run paywall are gone. The paywall still opens at the aha moment
  (`lib/daily-tasks/aha-paywall.ts`) and its other places.
- The picks button reads "Use these three" ("Use these two", "Use this one").
  Tapping it, "I'll add my own", or a dump with nothing to pick ends first run.
  Today then says "Your three are picked." (toast and VoiceOver), counting what
  actually went on today; if the day was locked meanwhile and none did, it says
  "Today is locked. N saved for later in Ideas." instead.
- A relaunch with tasks already on today but first run not marked seen (an
  older build's first run that stopped at its nudge step) just finishes.
- Analytics: `onboarding_step` now only has `dump`, `sorting`, `three`;
  `onboarding_completed` gains `count`, and `source` is `dump`, `own` or
  `resume` (tasks were already on today, nothing asked). New `first_days_card` with `kind`
  (`nudge` | `widget`), `action` (`shown` | `yes` | `not_now` | `dismissed`) and,
  for Yes, `outcome` (the permission answer). Ids and counts only.

**Today cards that replaced the last two screens** (`lib/daily-tasks/first-days.ts`,
`components/daily-tasks/first-days-cards.tsx`; own storage key `daily-tasks/first-days`).

- The record is written when first run ends, so only installs that went through
  the 1.5 first run see these cards. People updating were asked both in their
  own first run.
- "Want a nudge in the morning?" (Yes, remind me / Not now) shows on Today,
  under the coach's note, from the first check-off on the first-run day or from
  the next day, whichever comes first (never seconds after "picked"), only
  while iOS can still ask (permission undetermined): never when already
  granted, and not when denied (a Yes couldn't bring up the prompt). Never next
  to the rollover card, and not on a finished day (it would sit on the done card).
  Yes asks iOS and, when allowed, turns reminders and the morning one on; the
  card is then gone for good. Not now hides it for 3 days; the second Not now
  retires it (Settings › Reminders has everything).
- "Keep your three on your Home Screen" (iOS only) is earned on the first
  finished day (every planned task done, at least one), stamped with that app
  launch, and shows from a later launch (a new process) or a later day, never
  in the session that earned it; it stays until dismissed with ×. One card at a
  time: the nudge goes first.
- The cards' state lives in `useFirstDays` on Today, so layout changes never
  reload it; they render in one place, under the coach's note. "shown" goes out
  once per card per day, deduped in storage (`daily-tasks/first-days-shown`).
- "Reset all data" removes both keys.

**One word: lock.**

- Today: "Lock today" (lock icon) → "Lock today?" → Cancel / Lock. "Lock these
  three" in Ideas. A locked day reads "Today is locked. N to go." (or "Locked
  automatically at 12:00 PM. N to go.") with an inline **Unlock** on the same line.
  Unlock asks first: "Unlock today?", with Unlock the clear (preferred) action
  and Keep locked the cancel; the same dialog in Settings' "Unlock today" row,
  which stays. `day_unlocked` carries `source: "today"` or `"settings"`.
- Notes that said "Unlock it in Settings" now say "Unlock it", and every "set"
  that meant locked says "locked" (status, toasts, brain dump and Ideas notes,
  routines line, accessibility labels and hints, history's "Locked" pill).
  Settings: "Locking the day", "Lock the day automatically". Widgets and the Live
  Activity have no such strings. Internal names and ids (`lockToday`,
  `kind: "set"`, `setDay`) are unchanged.

**Auto-lock off for new installs.** `buildInitialState` uses
`NEW_INSTALL_AUTO_LOCK` (off, noon). Saved state is read as before
(`normalizeAutoLock`): a 1.4.x save has `enabled` written out and keeps it; a save
without it (or without `autoLock`) reads as on, what those builds ran with. So
nobody updating has it flipped; only a new install or "Reset all data" starts off
(and a corrupt save with no usable backup, which loads as a new install).

## 1.5 B: Plus moments

- **Evening teaser.** A free user who closes a perfect day (every task done, at least
  `EVENING_TEASER_MIN_DONE` = 3, in `lib/daily-tasks/evening-teaser.ts`) on a build that
  can reach the AI evening coach sees "Tomorrow could start with", one real line and two
  placeholder shapes, "Plus drafts a full three each night, from how today went. Your
  three stay free either way." and a secondary **Have my coach draft tomorrow** button
  (paywall source `evening_draft`). No AI call for free users, and nothing suggests a
  draft exists. The line comes from the device: the oldest task saved for later, else the
  built-in idea for the next step on the goal path; with none, the old "Tomorrow starts
  fresh." stays. Buying from it closes the day again with the AI coach; if that falls
  back with nothing drafted, "Couldn't draft tomorrow." with **Try again**. The check-in
  card, done card, "Tonight" line and "Drafting tomorrow…" (else "Closing the day…") only
  promise a coach's draft once Plus is confirmed and the coach is reachable.
- **Paywall.** Benefits lead with Nag mode, "Your coach drafts tomorrow", Smart brain dump,
  Break it down. Lifetime shows on every paywall once this install has closed two without
  buying (`LIFETIME_AFTER_DISMISSALS`, key `daily-tasks/plus-paywall-dismissals`; event
  `paywall_dismissed` with `source` and `count`). Only paywalls the user opened count
  (not onboarding, aha or win-back), and only once the plans had loaded. Never for a
  lapsed subscriber (an Apple billing retry could charge twice). Settings › Plus still
  always shows it. A feature's paywall leads with that feature's benefit.
- **One free Break it down.** Client: `FREE_BREAK_DOWNS` = 1 in `free-uses.ts` (claimed
  before the call, given back if the AI can't be reached or the network ceiling is full,
  set to used on a 402), "1 free try" next to the button, "Steps added. That was your one
  free try." after. While Plus is still being checked it's treated as the free try (given
  back if Plus is confirmed by the time it returns). Server: the break-down route allows
  a free id `FREE_BREAK_DOWNS_PER_CLIENT` (1) try ever, in the Limits object's new
  `once_counts` table, within `FREE_BREAK_DOWNS_PER_NETWORK_PER_DAY` (20) free tries per
  network a day (429 when only that is full, so the try isn't lost); a failed AI call
  gives the try back. A reinstall gets a new RevenueCat anonymous id and so one more try;
  the network and global caps bound that. Requests without an id stay under the shared
  no-id ceiling until `ENTITLEMENT_REQUIRE_ID=1`. The privacy policy now says a one-way
  record that the free try was used is kept (republish the gist).
- **Ship order:** deploy the Worker (`cd server && npx wrangler@4 deploy`) before the 1.5
  build goes out. With the old Worker the free try gets a 402 and the app shows the
  paywall, so nothing breaks, but the try doesn't work.

## 1.5 C: reasons under picks, smarter memory, "What your coach remembers"

**Reasons under picks.** The brain-dump route now returns a short reason per
pick (`reason`, at most five words and 40 characters, e.g. "Quick win",
"Been waiting a while"); the brain-dump review and first run's "Here are your
three" show it muted under each pick. A reason never states how many times a
task moved (it can read as blame, and could land on the wrong pick). Too
long, a link or bare domain, blame or shame, the task's own words, a count
of moves, or "waiting" on a pick the app didn't send as moved: dropped
(Worker and app). A pick the memory has as moved twice or more and no reason
gets "Been waiting a while"; the on-device split shows no reason. Not shown
on the Today card (the reason isn't stored with the task).

**Moved count.** Derived from history, no new task field: a past day a task
(same words, ignoring case and spacing) was on and not finished, after which
it came back (carried over, or on a later day's list in the window, today
included). Routine tasks don't count. "Not today" leaves no trace in history,
so it isn't counted.

**Basic memory (everyone).** `lib/daily-tasks/coach-memory.ts`, from the last
30 days before today: tasks finished of set, best weekdays (seen twice or
more, better than the overall rate and at least half done; up to two), tasks
moved twice or more (up to three), and kinds of task by first word (three or
more tasks: 80%+ done "usually get done", a third or less "tend to slip").
Nothing is stored; it is worked out each time.

**Sent to the AI.** `stats: { days, done, total, bestDays?, moved? }` with
brain dumps (free and Plus) and the plan request (next to the five-day
summary, which is unchanged). Nag lines get `moved` per task. A malformed
`stats` is ignored by the Worker, never a 400. Nothing new goes to analytics.
The privacy policy says so (the published gist copy needs the same update).

**What your coach remembers.** A Settings section for everyone: the basic
memory in plain sentences, led by what was finished; for Plus, the AI
coach's notes (`coachMemory`), which can be edited (Save/Cancel, 500
characters, with a count). "Clear memory" asks first, then empties the notes
and sets `memoryClearedAt` to today: the basic memory, the stats sent (with
a shorter `span`) and the nag weights look only at the days after it. A
plan or evening close already on its way is dropped / loses its memory. A
`memoryClearedAt` after today (clock ahead) is set to today at launch. The
history itself (calendar, streaks) is untouched.

**Nag mode leans on dodged tasks.** Built-in lines share their named lines by
weight 1 + times moved (at most 3), spread out by a smooth weighted round
robin; equal weights give exactly the old rotation. Times, counts and every
other rule are unchanged. The AI is told the move counts and asked to give
those tasks more lines.

**Deploy order.** Deploy the Worker before the app ships. An older Worker
already returns a `reason` on picks (its schema required one) but never
checked it; the app drops any that break the rules, but only the new Worker
asks for short ones and checks them against `stats`. The new Worker works
with older apps (they ignore `reason`).
