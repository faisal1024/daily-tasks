# 1.3: Routines (repeating tasks, suggested on Today and in Ideas)

Things you do on repeat ("Walk after lunch", "Inbox zero on Fridays").
Owner-approved shape: **a routine never fills a slot by itself; it is
suggested.** You still choose today's three.

Keep the app's tone: calm, no guilt. A routine that isn't picked on its day
shows nothing: no red, no "missed", no streak break, nothing overdue.

## Data

```ts
type Routine = {
  id: string;
  text: string;      // trimmed, at most 80 characters (like a typed task)
  days: number[];    // local weekdays, Sunday = 0; deduped and sorted, never empty
  paused: boolean;
  createdAt: string; // ISO
};
```

- Persisted in the store as `routines` (`AppState.routines`). Older saves
  have none and load as `[]`. Normalisation trims and caps the text, dedupes
  and sorts the days, and drops entries with no id, no text, no valid days,
  or a duplicate id (at most 50 kept).
- A task added from a routine carries an optional `routineId`. It is an
  ordinary task otherwise: the widget, Live Activity, history, rollover and
  focus timer treat it like any other.

Pure helpers live in `lib/daily-tasks/routines.ts`:
`routinesDueToday(routines, todayTasks, date)` returns active (not paused)
routines whose days include the date's local weekday and that aren't already
on today, either by `routineId` or by the same words (case- and
space-insensitive). Day presets: Every day, Weekdays (Mon–Fri), Weekends
(Sat, Sun), Custom days.

## Reducer actions

| Action | Rule |
| --- | --- |
| `addRoutine` | Needs text and at least one day. Refused at `max` (free: 2), at the 50 ceiling, and for the same words and days twice (a double-tapped Save: "exists"). |
| `updateRoutine` | Text and days; same validation. Tasks already added keep their words. |
| `setRoutinePaused` | Paused routines are never suggested. |
| `removeRoutine` | Only the suggestion goes; a task already added today stays. |
| `addRoutineToToday` | Same rules from Today's card and from Ideas: refused when today is set or has three tasks. Only a routine due today and not already on the list. |

## UI

- **Today** (added after TestFlight build 53, where a Weekdays routine was
  effectively invisible: Ideas' entry is hidden on a full or set day): a
  quiet "Today's routines" card sits directly under the task card (left
  column on iPad, above the day-5 trial note and the lower section) whenever
  `routinesDueToday` returns anything for today. Each row: repeat glyph, the
  words, and a compact "+ Add" (44 pt) that adds it like Ideas does
  (VoiceOver: "Added <text> to today"). Header "Today's routines" with an
  "Edit" link that opens the routines sheet. On a full day the Add buttons
  are off and one muted line says "Your three are picked. Free a slot to add
  one."; on a set day, "Today is set. Change it to add one." Once every task
  is done the card is hidden (no nagging). Only today: a past day's routines
  are never mentioned. Both places use the same helper, so an added routine
  leaves both at once.
- **Ideas sheet**: on a day with due routines, a "Today's routines" section
  sits at the top. One tap adds a routine to today (same affordance as an
  idea). When today is full or set, the rows are disabled and say why. On a
  day with none due, nothing shows. Not in the "Saved for later" view. An
  idea with the same words as a due routine is listed once, as the routine.
  With no routines at all, a quiet footer ("Do something on repeat? Make it
  a routine.") opens the routines sheet from Today.
- **Settings › Routines** (and that footer) opens a sheet to add (words + days: the presets
  and seven weekday toggles), edit, pause/resume and delete (with the usual
  confirmation). Empty state: "Things you do on repeat. They show on Today
  on their days — you choose if they make today's three."; with some, just
  "They show on Today on their days." After a save, a line under that
  routine (and a VoiceOver announcement) says when it shows: "Shows on Today
  on weekdays." / "…on Mondays and Thursdays." / "…every day."; when it's due
  today and today has room, "Shows on Today — you can add it now."; paused,
  "Paused. It won't show on Today until you resume it."
- **Plus**: free users keep up to 2 routines; adding a third opens the
  paywall (source `routines`, "Keep all your routines"); at the limit the
  sheet says so first ("Free keeps two routines. Plus keeps as many as you
  like."). After a purchase the sheet reopens in "New routine"; dismissed,
  it reopens on the list. Plus is unlimited.
  After Plus lapses, routines over the limit keep working; only new ones
  are blocked.

## Analytics

`routine_created` `{count}`, `routine_added_today` `{source: "today" | "ideas"}`, `routine_limit_hit`
`{count}` (tracked by the sheet when it opens the paywall, plus the usual
`plus_gate_hit` `{feature: "routines"}`). Routine text is never sent.
