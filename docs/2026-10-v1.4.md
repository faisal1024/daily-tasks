# 1.4

## Milestone suggestions

Owner's request: "Some of the suggestions should be based on my long-term
milestones. It should look at the next milestone and suggest something off of
that."

When there is a goal path with a milestone not yet reached, the Ideas sheet
shows a group **"Next on your path"** with the milestone's title and one or
two suggestions. It sits below the source chip and the hint, above the
general ideas, which are then headed **"More ideas"**. Free users get built-in suggestions (no AI, nothing leaves the
device); Plus users get AI ones.

Tone as everywhere: calm, no guilt, no hype. A suggestion that isn't picked
shows nothing. Nothing is marked missed or overdue.

### Which milestone

`nextIncompleteMilestone(milestones, completedMilestoneIds)`
(`lib/daily-tasks/milestones.ts`): the first one not reached. Reaching it
moves the group to the next one. No group when there is no path, when every
milestone is reached, or in the "Saved for later" view of the sheet.

### Built-in suggestions (free, and the fallback for Plus)

`lib/daily-tasks/milestone-ideas.ts` (pure, no react-native imports):

- `localMilestoneIdeas(milestone, today, lead?)` returns the four built-in
  lines in an order picked by the day and the milestone id, so the pair
  changes from day to day but not within a day:
  - `<title>: 10 easy minutes` (10 min)
  - `<title>: do the first small step` (5 min)
  - `<title>: 15 focused minutes` (15 min)
  - `<title>: plan the next small step` (5 min)
- The title leads and a small action follows, so the line reads as a task.
  Each line's time estimate matches what it says.
- On a starter path (`pathSource` "template": "Start small", "Repeat the
  rhythm", "Grow the challenge") the lines open with the goal's title
  instead, e.g. `Run a 5K: 10 easy minutes`.
- A task is at most 64 characters, so a long title is cut at a word and ends
  with "…" (`shortMilestoneTitle`), never through an emoji or an accented
  letter (`cutGraphemes`). Quote marks and closing punctuation are removed.
- Only the title is used. The description is sent to the AI (below) but not
  put into the built-in lines.

### AI suggestions (Plus)

The plan request (same route, same auth) now carries the next milestone, and
the response marks the suggestions made for it. Field details are in
`docs/momentum-ai-proxy.md` ("Next milestone").

- `requestMomentumAiPlan` sends `nextMilestone: { title, description? }`.
  Not with a fresh-path request, and not while the path is the starter one
  (`milestoneToAskAbout`): the AI's first plan replaces that path.
- Suggestions the proxy returns with `forMilestone: true` are saved with a
  `milestoneKey` (milestone id plus its title, `milestoneKey()`). The title is
  part of the key because ids are positional: after an edit or a new path the
  same id can name a different step, and an old suggestion must stop counting.
- When the plan is stored, a mark is kept only if it names the first open
  milestone of the path as stored (`settleMilestoneMarks`), so a path that
  was replaced or edited while the request was out never shows ideas made
  for another step.
- `buildMilestoneIdeaGroup` picks the group: up to two AI suggestions whose
  key matches the next open milestone (`source: "ai"`); otherwise up to two
  built-in ones (`source: "local"`).

The built-in ones show whenever there are no matching AI suggestions: a free
user, an older proxy that sends no mark, the model marking none, a failed AI
request (the app keeps or rebuilds the plan as before), a milestone just
reached, or a path edited since the plan was made.

### Rules in the sheet

- Same one-tap add as any other idea. Disabled when today is full or set. A
  suggestion already on today's list shows as added.
- An AI suggestion shown in the group is not listed again under the general
  ideas, and "Add all" only covers the general ideas (shown when two or more
  of them can still be added). With no general ideas left, there is no "More
  ideas" heading and no "Add all".
- A tapped suggestion whose words are already on today is refused by the
  store (`addSuggestions`), so a double tap adds once. Typing the same words
  by hand is still allowed.
- A built-in suggestion with the same words as a general idea or a routine due
  today is skipped, and the next built-in line takes its place.
- Accessibility: the group title is a header read as "Next on your path:
  <title>", said once; each row is a button labelled "Add <text>" (or
  "<text>, added") with nothing more about the milestone. "More ideas" is a
  header too.

### Today, midday

"Next on your path: <milestone>" still opens Progress. While there is room to
add (today isn't set or full), a second link under it, "Get ideas for this",
opens Ideas scrolled to the group.

### Analytics

`milestone_idea_added` with `source: "ai" | "local"`, sent when a suggestion
from the group lands on today (the store says so; a refused or repeated tap
is not counted). The milestone and task text are never sent.
