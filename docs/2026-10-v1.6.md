## 1.6 H: Sunday reset

A two-minute guided look at the week, Plus (`lib/daily-tasks/sunday-reset.ts`,
`components/daily-tasks/sunday-reset-sheet.tsx`, `sunday-reset-card.tsx`).

**Ways in.**

- Today, Sundays from 5 PM (`SUNDAY_RESET_CARD_HOUR`): "Set up your week" /
  "Two minutes: last week's wins, a dump for the week, and Monday's three."
  with **Start** and × ("Not this week"). Only for someone with a planned day
  before today (never on first run's day), never during first run, and not
  while Plus is still being checked. Gone once this week's reset is done or ×
  was tapped.
- Free users (a build with the paywall): the same card once, with a short
  Plus intro and **See Plus** (paywall source `sunday_reset`, leading with
  "Your coach drafts tomorrow"). It's stamped with its week
  (`freeIntroWeek`) and never shows in a later week. Buying from it opens the
  reset.
- Progress, under "Your last 7 days", any day: a "Set up your week" row (Plus;
  "Plus" for a free user, who gets the paywall). It plans the next day.
- The Sunday notification (Plus): "Sunday reset" / "Two minutes to set up your
  week?" at 6 PM on the next Sunday, with notifications on and allowed. Not
  for a week already done or closed with ×. One pending item, plain
  (`interruptionLevel: "active"`, never time-sensitive), no task words, and
  `fitPlanToLimit` still drops the latest first under iOS's 64. Settings ›
  Reminders › **Sunday reset** (Plus only) turns it off. Tapping it just opens
  the app (the card is there).

**The steps** (skip or close at any step; each step says "Step N of 3").

1. **Last week's wins**: the seven days ending today: tasks finished, days
   shown up, "Day N and counting." (the Day chip's N), and the best day when
   one clearly is (two or more done, no tie). No missed count, no "x of y".
   Nothing done: "A quiet week. A new one starts now."
2. **Dump for the week**: the brain dump's box, aimed at the week. **Next**
   (or **Skip** when empty). Everything from it goes to Saved for later at
   once. Closing with text typed saves the simple split of it, so nothing is
   lost.
3. **Monday's three** (the next day's weekday): the coach's suggestion,
   ticked, then the rest of the week's dump, saved items (newest first) and
   any draft already made for that day. Tick up to three, or **Add your own**.
   **Set Monday's three** (or "plan" for fewer) makes them the next day's draft;
   **Finish** with none ticked sets nothing. Then "Your week is set up."

**One AI call, the brain-dump route.** Step 2's Next sends the week's text,
the newest 20 saved items (`saved`), the basic memory's stats and `forDay`
(the weekday). With nothing typed, the saved items are the text. A pick that
is a saved item (same words, or clearly the same words reworded) is mapped
back to the saved words, so it leaves Saved for later the morning it's used
rather than sitting in both. No AI (no proxy, Plus not active) or a failure:
the dump's lines first, then the oldest saved items, with "Couldn't reach your
coach, so here's a simple start." when the AI should have answered.

**The draft.** The picks become `tomorrowDraft` with `from: "reset"` and
"You picked these when you set up your week." The next morning's card and
morning notification ("Your three for Monday") are the evening draft's own.
An evening close that night keeps the reset's draft (the user's own choice)
instead of drafting over it; the note is still saved. Used tasks leave Saved
for later as with any draft; dismissing it leaves them saved.

**State.** `AppState.sundayReset` (optional; older saves read as the
defaults): `reminder` (on), `doneWeek`, `dismissedWeek`, `freeIntroWeek`. A
week key is the Sunday that starts it, so a reset done any day of a week
counts for that week (Sunday's card and notification are for the next one).
"Reset all data" clears it with the rest of the state.

**Analytics.** `sunday_reset` with `step` (`card` | `intro` | `wins` | `dump` |
`three`), `action` (`shown` | `start` | `next` | `skip` | `saved` | `close` |
`dismissed` | `plus` | `set`), `source` (`today` | `progress`) and `count`
(items saved, or tasks set). Ids and counts only.

**Worker (deploy before the app ships).** The brain-dump route accepts two
optional fields: `forDay` (a weekday name: the prompt plans for that day, not
today) and `saved` (≤20 strings ≤120 chars: picks may come from them,
copying their words; never parked again, and the "from the dump" check
counts them). A bad one is a 400, like `agenda`. An older Worker ignores both
(picks "for today", saved items not considered); the app still works. Deploy
with `cd server && npx wrangler@4 deploy`. The privacy policy has a Sunday
reset line (republish the gist).

**Not yet.** Saved items get no dates from the week's dump: once 1.6 F
(#107, dated saved items) is on main, `saveForLater` in the sheet passes the
sort's dates to `parkTasks(texts, dates)` and the call sends `today`.
