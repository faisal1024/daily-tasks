## 1.6 E: capture from anywhere

A thought can be captured without first opening the app and finding the brain
dump. Free, no Plus gate. JS: `lib/daily-tasks/capture.ts` (links, parsing,
the toast), `lib/daily-tasks/capture-bridge.ts` (App Group keys), the store's
`syncCaptures`, Today's `dumpPrompt` / `captureNotice` effects.

**Two behaviours, on purpose:**

1. **Open the brain dump** — the Dump widget, the Dump control, the "Brain
   dump" shortcut and any `dailytasks://dump` link open the app on Today with
   the brain dump sheet up, ready to type. Nothing is saved until the user sorts
   or saves, exactly as when the dump is opened from Today.
2. **Save for later without opening the app** — the share sheet's "Three
   Today" and the "Save for later" shortcut ("Add to Three Today", with text)
   put the thought in a capture inbox in the App Group. The next time the app
   is in front (and onboarding is done), the captures go, oldest first, to
   **Saved for later** and Today says `Saved for later: “…”` (or "N thoughts
   saved for later"). Never onto today's three, and no sheet pops up
   uninvited.
   - **Saved for later is never pushed out.** Only as many captures as it
     has room for (50) come in; the rest wait in the inbox and Today says
     "Saved for later is full. Clear a few in Ideas to bring in the rest."
     (once per change in how many wait). The toast and `capture_imported`
     count only what was actually saved.
   - **The inbox itself is capped at 50 waiting captures.** A full inbox
     refuses a new capture and says so (Siri: "Your saved-for-later inbox is
     full. Open Three Today to sort it."; the share sheet shows an alert); it
     never drops an older one. A capture that can't be written says so too.

   *Why Saved for later, not a pre-filled brain dump:* it's durable (a
   pre-filled sheet loses the text if it's closed or the app is killed),
   it doesn't take over the screen when the app is opened for something
   else, and it's where things that aren't for today already live; the
   saved item can be swapped in or added later. Captures are made one line
   (whitespace collapsed) and cut at 500 characters; text already in Saved
   for later or on today isn't added twice.

**Native pieces** (all in the existing extensions except the share sheet):

| What | Where | Opens |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Dump widget, kind `ThreeTodayDump`: Lock Screen circular + rectangular, Home Screen small | `targets/widget/DumpWidget.swift` (widget extension) | `dailytasks://dump?source=widget` (widgetURL) |
| Dump control (iOS 18; Control Center, Lock Screen controls, Action button), kind `com.faisalislam.dailytasks.dump` | same file, `DumpControl` | `OpenDumpFromControlIntent` (source `widget`) |
| "Brain dump" App Shortcut (`BrainDumpIntent`) — "Brain dump in Three Today" | `targets/widget/CaptureIntents.swift` (app only) | the app, source `intent` |
| "Save for later" App Shortcut (`AddToThreeTodayIntent`; "Add to Three Today", "Save for later in Three Today"; a `Thought` text parameter, Siri asks "What's on your mind?") | same file (app only) | doesn't open the app; says "Saved for later." |
| Share extension "Three Today", target `ThreeTodayShare`, bundle id `com.faisalislam.dailytasks.share` | `targets/share/` (@bacons/apple-targets type `share`) | doesn't open the app |

- The shortcuts are listed in the app's one `AppShortcutsProvider`
  (`native/app/FocusShortcuts.swift`), so they can be bound to the Action
  button, Siri and Shortcuts. Without text, use "Brain dump"; with text, "Save
  for later" ("Add to Three Today").
- The share sheet is the system compose sheet ("Save for later in Three
  Today"). It shows exactly what will be saved, all editable: the shared text
  (plain-text attachments too) or the page's title, then the http(s) link.
  "Save" stays off until every attachment has loaded. It accepts text and one
  web URL only (no `TRUEPREDICATE`). It has its own privacy manifest
  (`targets/share/PrivacyInfo.xcprivacy`: no tracking, no data collected,
  UserDefaults reason `1C8F.1` for the App Group).
- `targets/share/CaptureInbox.swift` is the one App Group writer, compiled
  into the share extension, the widget extension (byte-identical copy in
  `targets/widget/`) and the app (`plugins/with-focus-app-intents.js` copies it,
  with `CaptureIntents.swift`, into the app target at prebuild).

**App Group keys** (`group.com.faisalislam.dailytasks`):

- `capture-inbox/` (a folder in the App Group container) — one JSON file
  per capture, `{text, at, source}`, named `<at ms>-<uuid>.json`, written
  atomically by native code, which only ever adds files (no lock, no shared
  list). The app lists them through the WidgetStorage module
  (`listInboxFiles` / `deleteInboxFile`), saves them oldest first by `at`,
  stores Saved for later on disk (`persistState`) and only then deletes
  each file. If storing or deleting fails the file is read again next time;
  it's then already saved, so nothing is added, said or counted twice.
  Malformed or empty files are deleted at once. Each capture keeps its `at`:
  dates in it (1.6 F) are read from the local day it was captured, against
  the store's day, so "call mum tomorrow" shared last night lands as today.
- `capture.openRequest` / `capture.openHandled` — "open the brain dump" from
  the shortcut or the control, for a cold start that misses the link (acted on
  within 60 s, once; the link carries the same id).

**When the app acts:** once ready and in front (never in a background launch:
"Add to Three Today" can run the app in the background), on each dump link,
and on every return to the front. The dump opens on a locked day too (the
sheet saves everything for later and says so), never over first run or the
paywall; anything else on screen (Ideas, routines, a focus screen, What's
new, the share preview, the perfect-day celebration) steps aside first.
**Every new sheet on Today must register in `stepAsideForDump`** (in
`app/(tabs)/index.tsx`): an entry that closes it if it's up and returns true.

**Analytics** (ids and counts only, never text): `capture_opened` with
`source` `widget` | `intent` | `link`; `capture_imported` with `count`.

**Release: one-time owner step.** The share extension is a new bundle id, so
it needs its own App ID and provisioning profile. The App Group already exists
and is reused.

1. Run one interactive build from `main` with your Apple login, once for
   **each build profile in use** (production, and preview or development if
   you use them):
   `npx eas-cli@latest build --platform ios --profile <profile>`
   (without `--non-interactive`; `npx eas-cli@latest credentials -p ios`
   works too). EAS reads the new target from
   `extra.eas.build.experimental.ios.appExtensions` (added by
   @bacons/apple-targets), registers `com.faisalislam.dailytasks.share`, turns
   on App Groups for it, and makes its distribution profile.
2. In the Apple Developer portal → Identifiers →
   `com.faisalislam.dailytasks.share`, check that App Groups is on with
   `group.com.faisalislam.dailytasks` ticked (EAS normally does this; if not,
   tick it and run `eas credentials` again to regenerate the profile).
3. Later builds are non-interactive again as before.

No new entitlements on the app or widget (the Dump widget and control live in
the existing widget extension), and no new Info.plist keys on the app.

## 1.6 F: smart resurfacing of dated items

A brain dump often says when ("deck for Tuesday", "passport before March").
Saved items now keep that date and come back on their day.

**The date** (`TaskWhen` in `lib/daily-tasks/types.ts`, logic in
`lib/daily-tasks/resurface.ts`): `{ date: "yyyy-MM-dd" (local), kind }`.
`on` is a day the item is for; `by` is a deadline, `date` being the last day
it can be done ("before March" → 28/29 Feb, "before Friday" → Thursday, "by
Friday" → Friday). Either way the item comes back the morning of `date`, so a
deadline comes back the morning before it passes. `ParkedTask.when` is
optional; old saves load without it, and a malformed one is dropped, never
the item (`normalizeParkedTasks`).

**Where dates come from.**

- *AI sort (Plus only).* The app sends `today` (local date key) with a Plus
  brain dump; the Worker asks for a nullable `when` on every pick and parked
  item and asks the model to keep the day words in that item's text ("Send
  the deck for Tuesday"). It passes a `when` on only when it's a real date,
  not before `today`, at most 366 days ahead, and **that item's own text**
  names a day or date (`DATE_CUE`: whole weekday and month names, an
  abbreviated month only with a dot or a day number, "May" only with a
  number, short weekdays only after by/on/before/due, today/tonight/tomorrow,
  next week/month, "3/14", "the 5th", "in two weeks"). That stops a date the
  model made up for an item with no day in it ("Call Mark", "Go to the
  market"); it can't prove a written day was read right, which is why every
  date can be changed or cleared by hand (below). The app checks again
  (`normalizeWhen`) and keeps them in `BrainDumpResult.dates`, keyed by the
  item's loose text key; Today hands them to `parkTasks`. Free sorts (the
  free tries and first run) send no `today`, so no AI dates.
- *On the device (everyone).* `parseLocalWhen`, run on every item as it is
  saved (`parkTasks` in `task-extras.ts`), when the AI gave none. Small and
  deterministic: "today", "tonight", "tomorrow"/"tmrw", weekday names,
  "next week" (next Monday); by/before/due make it a deadline ("until"/
  "till" don't: "Wait until Friday" is a day). Short forms ("fri", "Thurs.")
  only after on/by/before/until/due/this/next (not "for": "for sun", "Prep
  for SAT"), never in capitals. A bare weekday (or "this Tuesday") is the
  next one, today included; "next Tuesday" and "before Tuesday" on a Tuesday
  mean the week after. No date when two different days are named, after
  "every"/"each", for plural day names, "daily"/"weekly", a day gone by
  ("from/since/last/past Monday"), a possessive ("Tuesday's email"), a name
  ("Black Friday", "Thursday Murder Club", "Monday.com"), or when it lands
  before today. Months and numeric dates are left to the AI.
- *The same-day rule.* Something set aside today (left out of today's
  picks, a locked-day dump, "Not today", swapped out) isn't for today just
  because its words say "today" or the AI read today's weekday: a date equal
  to the day it's saved is dropped, AI or not.
- *Captures (for 1.6 E).* `parkTasks(texts, dates?, refDays?)` (store) /
  `parkTasks(state, texts, now, dates, { today, refDays })` (task-extras):
  `refDays[i]` is the day item `i` was written (yyyy-MM-dd). Its words are
  read against that day, the same-day rule doesn't apply (it was captured for
  that day), a date already past when it's imported is dropped, except a
  deadline missed in the last week, which comes in as Overdue.
- *Carried through Today.* A saved item added to Today (Add, Swap in, or
  from last night's draft) keeps its date on the task (`Task.when`, also on
  the day's history record, so a carried-over task keeps it through the
  rollover). "Not today" or being swapped out saves it again with that date
  as it is (`carried: true`; never re-read from the words): a deadline that
  has passed stays Overdue, and a deadline set aside on its own day keeps
  its date; only a plan set aside on its own day loses it. Editing a task's
  words drops its date (like its steps) and reads one from the new words.
- Dates are read against the store's day (`lastOpenedDate` / the action's
  `today`), not the clock's, so flying across a date line doesn't shift them.

**Showing it.** Saved for later shows a quiet line under a dated item:
"Today", "Tomorrow", "Tue" within the week, else "Mar 1" ("Mar 1, 2027" in
another year); a deadline reads "By Tue". Once the day has passed, a plan
(`on`) shows no date and simply drops back into the list; a deadline (`by`)
reads "Overdue" and leads for 7 days (`OVERDUE_DAYS`), also when the app
wasn't opened on the day, then its label goes. **Tapping the date** opens
"Change day" (iOS: a menu; Android: choices under the item): Today,
Tomorrow, the next five weekdays, or **No date** (`setParkedWhen`, store
action `setParkedWhen`); a deadline stays a deadline.

**Resurfacing.** Never adds anything to today by itself; the three-task
limit and the lock are untouched. Plans read "For today" / "For tomorrow" /
"For Tuesday"; only deadlines say "Due …" (or "Overdue").

- *Ideas (everyone).* Saved items for today (and deadlines missed in the
  last week) lead the sheet under a small "For today" heading, above routines
  and suggestions, with 1.5 C's muted reason line; the rest stay under
  "Saved for later". Under the heading an item shows its label ("Today",
  "By today", "Overdue"), not the reason again. The saved-only sheet lists
  them first, with the reason line. A locked or
  full day keeps them greyed out as before (Swap in still works on a full
  unlocked day). Its hint no longer says "Your day is full" when it isn't.
- *Today (everyone).* The "Saved for later (N)" link also shows when
  something leads, even with room: "Saved for later (3) · 1 for today".
- *Evening draft (Plus, like the coach's draft).* At the close, saved items
  for tomorrow are put first in tomorrow's draft (`withDueTomorrow`), still
  at most three (the AI's later picks make room; leftovers are on the
  rollover card as before). Done on the phone after the reply, so it works
  with the old Worker and with the on-device fallback; nothing new is sent to
  the evening route. The because line only speaks for what's in the draft:
  the coach's stays when all its picks made it, otherwise "Something you
  saved is for tomorrow." / "A few things you saved are for tomorrow.".
  "Try again" is judged on the coach's answer before saved items are added,
  so a failed AI close still offers it (under the draft: "The coach couldn't
  add to it."). `evening_closed` counts what the coach (or the on-device
  close) drafted, not the saved items added. Free users' on-device close is
  unchanged.
- *Morning card.* A drafted task that is a saved item for today shows "For
  today" ("Due today" for a deadline) under it (`draftDueReasons`); using it
  takes it out of Saved for later as before.

**Not done.** No "Plus also reads dates like 'March 1'" hint: it would be
one more line in a calm sheet, for little.

**Privacy and analytics.** No new analytics (none of the new paths send an
event). The only new thing sent is the local date with Plus brain dumps;
the privacy policy says so: "With Plus, your device's current date is sent
with the brain dump … Nothing else new is sent." (republish the gist).

**Compatibility.** New app ↔ old Worker: `today` is ignored, no `when` comes
back, dates come from the device only. Old app ↔ new Worker: no `today`, so
the prompt says "when is null" and any `when` is dropped; old apps ignore the
field anyway. The new schema requires a nullable `when` on every item (strict
OpenAI schemas need every property required). **Deploy the Worker before the
app ships** (`cd server && npx wrangler@4 deploy`) so Plus dumps get dates
from day one.

## 1.6 H: Sunday reset

A two-minute guided look at the week, Plus (`lib/daily-tasks/sunday-reset.ts`,
`components/daily-tasks/sunday-reset-sheet.tsx`, `sunday-reset-card.tsx`,
`sunday-reset-provider.tsx`, `lib/daily-tasks/sunday-reset-response.ts`).

**One sheet.** `SundayResetProvider` (at the tabs layout) renders the only
sheet and gives `isOpen`, `start(source)` and `stepAside()`. Today counts it
as something on screen (no rating ask, What's new or aha paywall over it),
steps it aside for a focus prompt, and registers it with 1.6 E's
`stepAsideForDump` so a Dump-widget tap closes it first. Stepping aside
closes it as × does, so a typed dump is saved.

**Ways in.**

- Today, Sundays from 5 PM (`SUNDAY_RESET_CARD_HOUR`): "Set up your week" /
  "Two minutes: your week's wins, a dump for the week, and Monday's three."
  with **Start** and × ("Not this week"). Only for someone with a planned day
  before today (never on first run's day), never during first run, and not
  while Plus is still being checked. Gone once this week's reset is done or ×
  was tapped.
- Free users (a build with the paywall): the same card once, with a short
  Plus intro, **See Plus** (paywall source `sunday_reset`, leading with the new
  "Sunday reset" benefit) and × ("Dismiss"). Stamped with its week
  (`freeIntroWeek`), never shown in a later week. Buying opens the reset.
- Progress, under "Your last 7 days", any day: "Set up your week" on Sundays,
  else "Plan tomorrow" / "Two minutes: your wins and tomorrow's three." ("Plus"
  for a free user, who gets the paywall). It plans the next day.
- The Sunday notification (Plus): "Your week" / "Two quiet minutes to set up
  Monday?" at 6 PM local on the next Sunday whose week isn't done or closed
  (finishing on a Sunday afternoon moves it to the Sunday after), with
  notifications on and allowed, and only where the card could show (after
  first run, with a planned day before today). One plain `active` item, never
  time-sensitive, no task words; `fitPlanToLimit` still drops the latest
  first. Settings › Reminders › **Sunday reset** (Plus) turns it off. A tap
  opens the reset itself (once the app has loaded, with Plus).

**The steps** (skip or close at any step; "Step N of 3" is shown and read out).

1. **Your week's wins**: the seven days ending today: tasks finished, days
   shown up, "Day N so far." and the best day when one clearly is. No missed
   count, no "x of y".
2. **Dump for the week**. Closing, or the app going to the background, while
   typing or while it sorts saves the typed text with the simple split
   (nothing lost); a later sort replaces those provisional items.
3. **Monday's three** (the planned day's weekday): saved items due that day
   (1.6 F) first and ticked, then the coach's suggestion, the rest of the
   week's dump, saved items, and a draft already made for that day. A saved
   item planned "on" a later day is offered but never ticked by default.
   Reopened after finishing, its own draft is ticked. At most eight rows, then
   "+N more in Saved for later". **Set Monday's plan**, or **Finish** with none.
   Done: "Monday's plan will be waiting in the morning. Everything else stays
   in Saved for later." / "Everything you added is in Saved for later." /
   "You're all set for the week.", and "We'll nudge you Sundays at 6 PM. Turn
   it off in Settings › Reminders." while that notification is on.

**One AI call, the brain-dump route**, 12 s at most (`WEEK_SORT_TIMEOUT_MS`,
then the simple split): the week's text, `today` (the store's day, 1.6 F),
`forDay`, the basic memory's stats and up to 20 saved items in resurfacing
order (due that day, due or overdue today, then oldest first; `savedForReset`).
With nothing typed, the saved items are the text. A pick that is a saved item
(the same words, or clearly reworded) is mapped back to the saved words. The
AI's dates go to `parkTasks(texts, dates)`; the simple split's are read on the
device.

**Saved for later** holds 100 (`MAX_PARKED_TASKS`, was 50). The reset saves
only what fits and says "N didn't fit. Saved for later is full."; it never
pushes the oldest out.

**The draft.** The picks become `tomorrowDraft` with `from: "reset"` and
"You picked these when you set up your week.", for the day planned when it
sorted (finishing after midnight still fills Monday). The morning card and
notification ("Your three for Monday", calm or Nag) are the evening draft's.
An evening close that night keeps it. Used tasks leave Saved for later.

**State.** `AppState.sundayReset` (optional; defaults when absent):
`reminder` (on), `doneWeek`, `dismissedWeek`, `freeIntroWeek`. A week key is
the Sunday that starts it. "Reset all data" clears it.

**Analytics.** `sunday_reset` with `step` (`card` | `intro` | `wins` | `dump` |
`three`), `action` (`shown` | `start` | `next` | `skip` | `saved` | `close` |
`dismissed` | `plus` | `set`), `source` (`today` | `progress` |
`notification`) and `count`. Ids and counts only.

**Worker (deploy before the app ships).** The brain-dump route accepts
optional `forDay` (a weekday name) and `saved` (≤20 strings, ≤120 whole
characters each, counted as the app counts them). Saved items are never parked
again (judged on the model's words before shortening, after 1.6 F's dates are
mapped), and count in the "from the dump" check. A bad value is a 400. An
older Worker ignores both; the app still works. `wrangler deploy --dry-run`
passes; deploy with `cd server && npx wrangler@4 deploy`. The privacy policy
lists what the reset sends (republish the gist).

**With 1.6 E.** The inbox's cap (50) is its own, separate from Saved for
later's; captures waiting for room come in as soon as room is made; an inbox
file that can't be read is skipped (read again later), and only invalid JSON
is deleted.
