## 1.6 G: your own nag lines; Mark done and Snooze on nags

Plus, Nag mode only. Files: `lib/daily-tasks/nag-own-lines.ts` (new, pure),
`lib/daily-tasks/nag-plan.ts` (`nagSlotWords`' own-line overlay,
`snoozeNagRecord`, the categories), `lib/daily-tasks/nag-day.ts`
(`snoozeNagDay`), `lib/daily-tasks/notifications.ts` (buttons, parsing),
`lib/daily-tasks/store.tsx` (`setNagOwnLines`, the button handler),
`components/daily-tasks/nag-own-lines.tsx` (Settings).

### In your own words

Settings › Reminders, with Nag on, under Sample: **In your own words**. Up to
5 lines (`MAX_NAG_OWN_LINES`), 80 characters each
(`MAX_NAG_OWN_LINE_CHARS`), added one at a time and removed with ×. Saved in
`notifications.nagOwnLines` (app state, on the phone). Not in the "Meet Nag
mode" sheet: the sheet is an intro before Nag is on, and the lines are only
used once it is.

- **Never sent anywhere.** Not to the AI service (the request still carries
  only the open tasks, the voice, the level and the count), not to analytics
  (no event and no count for them). "Reset all data" clears them with the
  other settings. Calm keeps them (they are settings, not the day's record).
- **Checks** (`checkNagOwnLine`, on Add and again when saved state loads):
  cleaned as an AI line is (one line; invisible format characters, bidi marks,
  overrides and isolates and zero-width characters taken out; control
  characters become spaces), not empty, at most 80 characters, no link
  (the AI lines' pattern), `{task}` at most once and no other braces, no two
  the same (any case). The voices' blocklists are **not** applied (no
  affection check, no Drill sergeant rudeness rule, no clock words): they are
  the user's own words, for their own phone, and they wrote them. Nothing a
  user line says is ever sent, so the proxy's rules don't apply either.
- **`{task}`**: the name of the task the nag is about, cleaned and quoted like
  a built-in line's (`nagTaskName`). The slot's own task when it is still open
  and not sensitive, else the first open task that isn't sensitive; with none,
  that line isn't used for that nag. A sensitive task (`nagTaskIsSensitive`)
  is never put in one.
- **Hide task names**: a line with `{task}` isn't used (Settings says so under
  the lines). A line without it is still used, unless it holds the words of
  one of the open tasks (three characters or more, any case:
  `nagOwnLineHoldsTask`), so it can't put a task's name on the lock screen.
  Such a nag carries no `taskId`, as every nag with names hidden.
- **How many, and where** (`nagOwnSteps`): at most one nag in three
  (`floor(total / 3)` of the day's steps, and no more nags than lines), never
  two in a row. The steps are marked on the slots when the day is planned
  (`NagSlot.own`, drawn as for five lines), so adding or removing a line
  mid-day doesn't move them, and a nag that went off at one counts as own
  without waiting for the write-back (review fix). The day's steps are cut into that many equal
  parts (three steps or more each) and each part's step is drawn, seeded by the
  day and the salt, from all but its last step, so two are always at least two
  steps apart. Gentle 4 → 1, Firm 7 → up to 2, Relentless 11 → up to 3. A
  day planned with one or two nags left has none. Two own lines in a row are
  still avoided after a snooze took a nag out between them: the overlay also
  checks the nag before (a past one counts as own when it was at an own
  step).
- **About a done task**: a line holding a distinctive word
  (`nagDistinctiveWords`) of a task ticked or removed today isn't used
  ("Dentist. Now." once the dentist is done). Settings nudges people to
  `{task}` instead. A filled line is cut to 140 characters (`MAX_NAG_LINE`).
- **Which line**: the k-th own step starts at the k-th line and takes the first
  one not said yet today, so each line is said at most once a day, and the
  answer is the same on every sync. With no unsaid line left the nag says what
  it would have said anyway.
- **Title**: the chosen voice's (`nagTitle`, "Three Today · Mom").
- **How it's put in**: when the plan is made (`nagSlotWords`), not stored as
  the slot's `text`. So the AI's lines never overwrite one, editing the lines
  takes effect at the next sync without planning the day again, and the 1.4.1
  write-back notes it as the slot's `sent`, so once that nag has gone off the
  line counts as said ("never the same nag twice", a redraw doesn't repeat it).
- Without Plus the saved lines are kept and unused (Nag mode doesn't apply).

### Mark done, Snooze 1 hour, No more nags today

Two categories now (re-registered at every launch, through the category
queue, so a nag scheduled by 1.5 gets the new buttons):

| Category | Nags | Buttons |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `three-today:nag` (`NAG_CATEGORY_ID`) | about a task (`data.taskId`) | Mark done, Snooze 1 hour, No more nags today |
| `three-today:nag-general` (`NAG_GENERAL_CATEGORY_ID`) | about the list in general, and every nag with names hidden | Snooze 1 hour, No more nags today |

The stop button was "Stop for today" (1.4, 1.5); same id (`nag-stop-today`),
same action. A nag pending from 1.5 with the old category is scheduled
again with the new one (`alreadyPending` compares `categoryIdentifier`).

- The ticking button says **Mark done**, as on the focus timer's notification
  and the Live Activity (the owner's rule: never a bare "Done"). Decided: a nag
  with no task has no Mark done, rather than ticking "the next" task the user
  can't see named.
- All buttons open the app (`opensAppToForeground`), like the stop button and
  the focus buttons: a background action is lost when the app isn't running.
  A tap that launched the app is applied once saved state has loaded. Only for
  a nag of today (its id's day); an older one's tap does nothing, except Mark
  done on a task that is still open today (last night's nag, tapped after
  midnight).
- **Mark done** ticks the task in the nag's data, as the focus notification's
  Mark done does (goes to Today; `task_completed { source: "notification" }`);
  nothing when it is already ticked or gone. It never unticks
  (`completeTask`, not a toggle), so two taps handled together tick once. The tick rewrites the reminders,
  so the day's nags follow (its nags point at the next open task, or stop when
  none is left). A nag scheduled by 1.5 about the list in general has the
  task category and no `taskId`: its Mark done just opens Today.
- **Snooze 1 hour** (`snoozeNagRecord`, decided: "the next nag comes an hour
  after the tap", rather than only cancelling what falls in that hour, which on
  a Gentle day would usually do nothing):
  - the first nag still to come with words is moved to the tap + 60 minutes
    (whole minute) when it was sooner; one planned for later keeps its time
    (review fix: a snooze never brings a nag sooner). It keeps its id, step
    and words (the plan works its words out as usual, so a ticked task,
    hidden names and own lines still apply);
  - every other nag still to come that would be less than an hour after it is
    taken out of the day's record, so the hour between nags holds; later ones
    keep their times;
  - when tap + 60 minutes is outside the window, nothing is moved and every nag
    before it is taken out (quiet for the hour, or for the rest of the window);
  - `snoozedUntil` is kept in the record: a day drawn again later today
    (another level or window) starts no earlier than that, and the plan never
    schedules a nag before it. It survives a Calm stub; a new day drops it.
  - Queued behind `ensureNagDay`'s runs, so a request for lines in flight is
    saved first; nothing after "Reset all data" or Calm since it was asked.
    The version goes up, so the reminders are written again. The nags due
    within the hour are taken off the phone at once
    (`cancelPendingNagsBefore`), so none goes off while the snooze waits
    behind a request for lines.
  - Today then says "Next nag around 3:40 PM", or "No more nags today (past
    your nag hours)" (`nagSnoozeNote`). The tap opens Today for that.
- **No more nags today** (was "Stop for today") does what it did.
- **Never time-sensitive**: the categories add buttons only; every nag is still
  `interruptionLevel: "active"` and silent.
- **Analytics**: `nag_action { action: "done" | "snooze" | "stop" }` for every
  button tap on a nag of today (ids only); Stop also still sends
  `nag_paused_today { source: "notification" }`.

### Nags that fit the actual task (owner: "sometimes it doesn't make sense")

Causes found, and what changed:

1. **The day's tasks key knew the tasks' words, not which task had them**
   (`nagTasksKey`: a set of hashes of the words). Two tasks that swapped their
   words, or a task given words another one had (Swap in with a saved item's
   text), looked unchanged: the AI's lines and the slots kept pointing at the
   task id they were written for, so a line about A went out on B (and its
   Mark done would tick B). Now each entry is a hash of the task's id and its
   words together, so that is a change: the day is planned again and the AI's
   lines for the changed tasks aren't kept (see 6). A record saved before 1.6 is planned again once at the
   first sync of the day (its key knows no task); that may use the day's
   second AI request.
2. **Between an edit and the day being planned again**, the sync could write
   the old words (the plan reads the saved record; a failed save left it so).
   `nagSlotWords` now treats a slot about an open task whose words changed
   since the record was written (`tasksKey` doesn't know that id with those
   words) like a ticked task's: it says a built-in line about the first open
   task that isn't sensitive, as it is now, with that task's id.
3. **The AI could name a task loosely, or not at all, or another one.** The
   prompt asked for "a short name taken from its text (two to five words)",
   so lines paraphrased ("the walk" for "20-minute easy run") and a line
   could carry one task's `taskIndex` while talking about another. The prompt
   now asks for the task quoted exactly (first whole words of a long one),
   fitted to what kind of task it is (a call, an email, a workout, a chore,
   an appointment), never implying another kind, never naming another task,
   and general lines that don't count the tasks ("the list", not "all
   three"). The proxy (`sanitizeNagLines`) and the phone (`parseNagResponse`)
   drop a line that doesn't name its task (quoted or whole words), quotes or
   names another task, and a general line that names a task (so a general
   line can't name a task that has since been ticked). `nagLineFitsTask` in
   `nag.ts` and in `nag-contract.mjs`; a test runs both on the same cases.
   Until the Worker is deployed, an older proxy's paraphrased lines are dropped
   on the phone, and with fewer than three left the day keeps its built-in
   lines.
4. **Built-in lines that assumed a kind of task**: "{task} reports for duty"
   and "Roll call. {task} is still present" (a person), "{task} is still
   standing" (a thing), "Lace up. {task}…", "{task} is your next rep", "One
   good rep on {task}", "No more stretching" (a workout), "Mission briefing:
   {task}. Simple." (simple). Rewritten: "Fall in, recruit. On the board:
   {task}.", "Roll call. {task}: still not ticked, recruit.", "Recruit! {task}
   is still open. Fix that.", "Game face on. {task} is a great place to
   start.", "Nice and steady. {task} is up next.", "One good push on {task}
   and today counts.", "No more stalling. {task} is next. Let's go." (was "Time to move on {task}"), "Mission
   briefing: {task}. Doable. Yours.". Two general lines that assumed several
   open tasks ("Pick any one to start", "Pick an item… Any item") now read
   "Start anywhere on the list" and "Pick the next item and move out. Now.".
   Counts, stages, the rude lines and every rule test are unchanged. A test
   fills every `{task}` line with "Call the dentist", "Taxes", "20-minute
   easy run", "Reply to Sam", "Groceries" and "Email J.R." and runs the task
   check on it, both sides.
5. Checked and unchanged: a slot about a ticked or removed task is pointed at
   the next open one with a built-in line for it (1.4); "Hide task names"
   gives general lines with no task id; a reorder changes no nag. A day with
   one open task still has one general line in each third of two or more
   nags: that is the 1.4 rule ("How often a built-in nag names a task"), and
   the general lines no longer assume more than one task.

6. **Review fixes (PR 109)**:
   - **One word is enough to be about a task.** `nagLineFitsTask` (both
     sides, the same code and the same `NAG_STOPWORDS`) also drops a task's
     line holding a distinctive word of another task, and a general line
     holding one of any task: "Don't forget the dentist.", "“Walk the dog”
     before the dentist closes." (for the walk), "Your report is waiting. So
     is the dog.". A distinctive word is four letters or more, not all
     digits, not a stopword, plural "s" off. The stopwords hold function
     words and the verbs and nouns any task has (make, work, read, list,
     call, send, check, finish, start, today, done, task, thing, stuff,
     time, email...), so a generic word never drops a line. The phone checks
     against every open task, also one past the three sent.
   - **A done task's words, after the fact.** The record keeps hashes of
     each planned task's distinctive words (`taskWords`, never the words).
     At plan time an AI line, general ones included, holding a word of a
     task since ticked or removed (a word an open task shares doesn't count)
     gets the built-in stand-in a ticked task's slot gets.
   - **One edit keeps the other lines.** After an edit only the edited
     task's AI lines go (and any line that no longer reads right with the
     tasks as they are); the rest keep their steps. `linesFor` pins the tasks
     the lines were written for, so a failed request since (which moves
     `aiFor`) can't bring an edited task's old lines back.
   - Built-in lines that assumed a kind of task rewritten: "Small list
     today. I've mowed bigger lawns.", "Are you doing {task}...", "I put
     {task} on the list...", "Time to move on {task}", "Leave it all out
     there", "DROP AND GIVE ME TEN MINUTES" and Coach's "Water break's
     over".
   - The prompt says a task may be a noun ("Dentist") or a command ("Call
     mom"), to quote it exactly, to match the nag to the task's effort, and
     never to use a word of another task.

Test fix: `tests/ui/nag-hardening.test.tsx`'s `LINE_WORDS` and `TASK_WORDS`
match whole words only, so a random salt, hash or analytics id that happens
to hold "babe" or "champ" no longer fails the "nothing leaks" checks.

### Release checklist (hard steps)

1. **Deploy the Worker before shipping the app**: `cd server && npx
   wrangler@4 deploy`, then check `/api/momentum/nag-lines` answers with the
   new rules. Until it is deployed, the phone drops the old proxy's lines
   that name another task or paraphrase, and a day with fewer than 3 usable
   lines keeps its built-in lines.
2. Only then submit the 1.6 build.

### Limits

- A snoozed nag that was moved keeps its id, whose `HHmm` is its old time
  (ids only need to be unique and carry the day).
- A nag that went off at an own step counts as own even when it said
  something else (no line was left): the next own line may wait one more nag.
- Snooze taken when the app can't save the record (storage failing) does
  nothing; the nags stay as they were.

## 1.6 F: smart resurfacing of dated items

A brain dump often says when ("deck for Tuesday", "passport before March").
Saved items now keep that date and come back on their day.

**The date** (`TaskWhen` in `lib/daily-tasks/types.ts`, logic in
`lib/daily-tasks/resurface.ts`): `{ date: "yyyy-MM-dd" (local), kind }`.
`on` is a day the item is for; `by` is a deadline, `date` being the last day
it can be done ("before March" → 28/29 Feb, "before Friday" → Thursday, "by
Friday" → Friday). Either way the item comes back the morning of `date`, so a
deadline comes back the morning before it passes. `ParkedTask.when` is
optional; old saves load without it, and a malformed one is dropped, never
the item (`normalizeParkedTasks`).

**Where dates come from.**

- *AI sort (Plus only).* The app sends `today` (local date key) with a Plus
  brain dump; the Worker asks for a nullable `when` on every pick and parked
  item and asks the model to keep the day words in that item's text ("Send
  the deck for Tuesday"). It passes a `when` on only when it's a real date,
  not before `today`, at most 366 days ahead, and **that item's own text**
  names a day or date (`DATE_CUE`: whole weekday and month names, an
  abbreviated month only with a dot or a day number, "May" only with a
  number, short weekdays only after by/on/before/due, today/tonight/tomorrow,
  next week/month, "3/14", "the 5th", "in two weeks"). That stops a date the
  model made up for an item with no day in it ("Call Mark", "Go to the
  market"); it can't prove a written day was read right, which is why every
  date can be changed or cleared by hand (below). The app checks again
  (`normalizeWhen`) and keeps them in `BrainDumpResult.dates`, keyed by the
  item's loose text key; Today hands them to `parkTasks`. Free sorts (the
  free tries and first run) send no `today`, so no AI dates.
- *On the device (everyone).* `parseLocalWhen`, run on every item as it is
  saved (`parkTasks` in `task-extras.ts`), when the AI gave none. Small and
  deterministic: "today", "tonight", "tomorrow"/"tmrw", weekday names,
  "next week" (next Monday); by/before/due make it a deadline ("until"/
  "till" don't: "Wait until Friday" is a day). Short forms ("fri", "Thurs.")
  only after on/by/before/until/due/this/next (not "for": "for sun", "Prep
  for SAT"), never in capitals. A bare weekday (or "this Tuesday") is the
  next one, today included; "next Tuesday" and "before Tuesday" on a Tuesday
  mean the week after. No date when two different days are named, after
  "every"/"each", for plural day names, "daily"/"weekly", a day gone by
  ("from/since/last/past Monday"), a possessive ("Tuesday's email"), a name
  ("Black Friday", "Thursday Murder Club", "Monday.com"), or when it lands
  before today. Months and numeric dates are left to the AI.
- *The same-day rule.* Something set aside today (left out of today's
  picks, a locked-day dump, "Not today", swapped out) isn't for today just
  because its words say "today" or the AI read today's weekday: a date equal
  to the day it's saved is dropped, AI or not.
- *Captures (for 1.6 E).* `parkTasks(texts, dates?, refDays?)` (store) /
  `parkTasks(state, texts, now, dates, { today, refDays })` (task-extras):
  `refDays[i]` is the day item `i` was written (yyyy-MM-dd). Its words are
  read against that day, the same-day rule doesn't apply (it was captured for
  that day), a date already past when it's imported is dropped, except a
  deadline missed in the last week, which comes in as Overdue.
- *Carried through Today.* A saved item added to Today (Add, Swap in, or
  from last night's draft) keeps its date on the task (`Task.when`, also on
  the day's history record, so a carried-over task keeps it through the
  rollover). "Not today" or being swapped out saves it again with that date
  as it is (`carried: true`; never re-read from the words): a deadline that
  has passed stays Overdue, and a deadline set aside on its own day keeps
  its date; only a plan set aside on its own day loses it. Editing a task's
  words drops its date (like its steps) and reads one from the new words.
- Dates are read against the store's day (`lastOpenedDate` / the action's
  `today`), not the clock's, so flying across a date line doesn't shift them.

**Showing it.** Saved for later shows a quiet line under a dated item:
"Today", "Tomorrow", "Tue" within the week, else "Mar 1" ("Mar 1, 2027" in
another year); a deadline reads "By Tue". Once the day has passed, a plan
(`on`) shows no date and simply drops back into the list; a deadline (`by`)
reads "Overdue" and leads for 7 days (`OVERDUE_DAYS`), also when the app
wasn't opened on the day, then its label goes. **Tapping the date** opens
"Change day" (iOS: a menu; Android: choices under the item): Today,
Tomorrow, the next five weekdays, or **No date** (`setParkedWhen`, store
action `setParkedWhen`); a deadline stays a deadline.

**Resurfacing.** Never adds anything to today by itself; the three-task
limit and the lock are untouched. Plans read "For today" / "For tomorrow" /
"For Tuesday"; only deadlines say "Due …" (or "Overdue").

- *Ideas (everyone).* Saved items for today (and deadlines missed in the
  last week) lead the sheet under a small "For today" heading, above routines
  and suggestions, with 1.5 C's muted reason line; the rest stay under
  "Saved for later". Under the heading an item shows its label ("Today",
  "By today", "Overdue"), not the reason again. The saved-only sheet lists
  them first, with the reason line. A locked or
  full day keeps them greyed out as before (Swap in still works on a full
  unlocked day). Its hint no longer says "Your day is full" when it isn't.
- *Today (everyone).* The "Saved for later (N)" link also shows when
  something leads, even with room: "Saved for later (3) · 1 for today".
- *Evening draft (Plus, like the coach's draft).* At the close, saved items
  for tomorrow are put first in tomorrow's draft (`withDueTomorrow`), still
  at most three (the AI's later picks make room; leftovers are on the
  rollover card as before). Done on the phone after the reply, so it works
  with the old Worker and with the on-device fallback; nothing new is sent to
  the evening route. The because line only speaks for what's in the draft:
  the coach's stays when all its picks made it, otherwise "Something you
  saved is for tomorrow." / "A few things you saved are for tomorrow.".
  "Try again" is judged on the coach's answer before saved items are added,
  so a failed AI close still offers it (under the draft: "The coach couldn't
  add to it."). `evening_closed` counts what the coach (or the on-device
  close) drafted, not the saved items added. Free users' on-device close is
  unchanged.
- *Morning card.* A drafted task that is a saved item for today shows "For
  today" ("Due today" for a deadline) under it (`draftDueReasons`); using it
  takes it out of Saved for later as before.

**Not done.** No "Plus also reads dates like 'March 1'" hint: it would be
one more line in a calm sheet, for little.

**Privacy and analytics.** No new analytics (none of the new paths send an
event). The only new thing sent is the local date with Plus brain dumps;
the privacy policy says so: "With Plus, your device's current date is sent
with the brain dump … Nothing else new is sent." (republish the gist).

**Compatibility.** New app ↔ old Worker: `today` is ignored, no `when` comes
back, dates come from the device only. Old app ↔ new Worker: no `today`, so
the prompt says "when is null" and any `when` is dropped; old apps ignore the
field anyway. The new schema requires a nullable `when` on every item (strict
OpenAI schemas need every property required). **Deploy the Worker before the
app ships** (`cd server && npx wrangler@4 deploy`) so Plus dumps get dates
from day one.
