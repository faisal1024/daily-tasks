## 1.6 F: smart resurfacing of dated items

A brain dump often says when ("deck for Tuesday", "passport before March").
Saved items now keep that date and come back on their day.

**The date** (`TaskWhen` in `lib/daily-tasks/types.ts`, logic in
`lib/daily-tasks/resurface.ts`): `{ date: "yyyy-MM-dd" (local), kind }`.
`on` is a day the item is for; `by` is a deadline, `date` being the last day
it can be done ("before March" → 28/29 Feb, "before Friday" → Thursday, "by
Friday" → Friday). Either way the item comes back the morning of `date`, so a
deadline comes back the morning before it passes. `ParkedTask.when` is
optional; old saves load without it, and a malformed one is dropped, never
the item (`normalizeParkedTasks`).

**Where dates come from.**

- *AI sort (Plus only).* The app sends `today` (local date key) with a Plus
  brain dump; the Worker asks for a nullable `when` on every pick and parked
  item and passes one on only when it's a real date, not before `today`, at
  most 366 days ahead, and the dump names some day or date at all (never an
  invented one). The app checks again (`normalizeWhen`) and keeps them in
  `BrainDumpResult.dates`, keyed by the item's loose text key; the Today
  screen hands them to `parkTasks` for what's saved. Free sorts (the free
  tries and first run) send no `today`, so no AI dates.
- *On the device (everyone).* `parseLocalWhen`, run on every item as it is
  saved (`parkTasks` in `task-extras.ts`), when the AI gave none. Small and
  deterministic: "today", "tonight", "tomorrow"/"tmrw", weekday names
  (short forms like "fri" only after on/for/by/before/until/due/this/next),
  "next week" (next Monday); by/before/until/till/due make it a deadline.
  A bare weekday (or "this Tuesday") is the next one, today included; "next
  Tuesday" and "before Tuesday" on a Tuesday mean the week after. No date
  when two different days are named, after "every"/"each", for plural day
  names ("Mondays"), "daily"/"weekly", or when it lands before today. A date
  that is the very day it's saved is ignored on this path: something set
  aside today ("Not today", or left out of today's picks) isn't due today
  just because its words say "today". Months and numeric dates are left to
  the AI.

**Showing it.** Saved for later shows a quiet line under a dated item:
"Today", "Tomorrow", "Tue" within the week, else "Mar 1"; a deadline reads
"By Tue". A day already past always reads "Oct 20" (a bare weekday would
read as the coming one), and it no longer leads.

**Resurfacing.** Never adds anything to today by itself; the three-task
limit and the lock are untouched.

- *Ideas (everyone).* Saved items due today lead the sheet, above routines
  and suggestions, with 1.5 C's muted reason line "Due today"; the rest stay
  under "Saved for later". The saved-only sheet lists them first. A locked
  or full day keeps them greyed out as before (Swap in still works on a full
  unlocked day). Its hint no longer says "Your day is full" when it isn't.
- *Today (everyone).* The "Saved for later (N)" link also shows when a saved
  item is due today, even with room: "Saved for later (3) · 1 due today".
- *Evening draft (Plus, like the coach's draft).* At the close, saved items
  due tomorrow are put first in tomorrow's draft (`withDueTomorrow`), still at
  most three (the AI's later picks make room; leftovers are on the rollover
  card as before). Done on the phone after the reply, so it works with the
  old Worker and with the on-device fallback; nothing new is sent to the
  evening route. A draft that was otherwise empty says "Something you saved
  is due tomorrow." (and then there's no "Couldn't draft tomorrow" retry).
  Free users' on-device close is unchanged.
- *Morning card.* A drafted task that is a saved item due today shows "Due
  today" under it (`draftDueReasons`); using it takes it out of Saved for
  later as before.

**Privacy and analytics.** No new analytics (none of the new paths send an
event). The only new thing sent is the local date with Plus brain dumps;
the privacy policy says so (republish the gist).

**Compatibility.** New app ↔ old Worker: `today` is ignored, no `when` comes
back, dates come from the device only. Old app ↔ new Worker: no `today`, so
the prompt says "when is null" and any `when` is dropped; old apps ignore the
field anyway. The new schema requires a nullable `when` on every item (strict
OpenAI schemas need every property required). **Deploy the Worker before the
app ships** (`cd server && npx wrangler@4 deploy`) so Plus dumps get dates
from day one.
