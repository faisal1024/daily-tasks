## 1.6 F: smart resurfacing of dated items

A brain dump often says when ("deck for Tuesday", "passport before March").
Saved items now keep that date and come back on their day.

**The date** (`TaskWhen` in `lib/daily-tasks/types.ts`, logic in
`lib/daily-tasks/resurface.ts`): `{ date: "yyyy-MM-dd" (local), kind }`.
`on` is a day the item is for; `by` is a deadline, `date` being the last day
it can be done ("before March" → 28/29 Feb, "before Friday" → Thursday, "by
Friday" → Friday). Either way the item comes back the morning of `date`, so a
deadline comes back the morning before it passes. `ParkedTask.when` is
optional; old saves load without it, and a malformed one is dropped, never
the item (`normalizeParkedTasks`).

**Where dates come from.**

- *AI sort (Plus only).* The app sends `today` (local date key) with a Plus
  brain dump; the Worker asks for a nullable `when` on every pick and parked
  item and asks the model to keep the day words in that item's text ("Send
  the deck for Tuesday"). It passes a `when` on only when it's a real date,
  not before `today`, at most 366 days ahead, and **that item's own text**
  names a day or date (`DATE_CUE`: whole weekday and month names, an
  abbreviated month only with a dot or a day number, "May" only with a
  number, short weekdays only after by/on/before/due, today/tonight/tomorrow,
  next week/month, "3/14", "the 5th", "in two weeks"). That stops a date the
  model made up for an item with no day in it ("Call Mark", "Go to the
  market"); it can't prove a written day was read right, which is why every
  date can be changed or cleared by hand (below). The app checks again
  (`normalizeWhen`) and keeps them in `BrainDumpResult.dates`, keyed by the
  item's loose text key; Today hands them to `parkTasks`. Free sorts (the
  free tries and first run) send no `today`, so no AI dates.
- *On the device (everyone).* `parseLocalWhen`, run on every item as it is
  saved (`parkTasks` in `task-extras.ts`), when the AI gave none. Small and
  deterministic: "today", "tonight", "tomorrow"/"tmrw", weekday names,
  "next week" (next Monday); by/before/due make it a deadline ("until"/
  "till" don't: "Wait until Friday" is a day). Short forms ("fri", "Thurs.")
  only after on/by/before/until/due/this/next (not "for": "for sun", "Prep
  for SAT"), never in capitals. A bare weekday (or "this Tuesday") is the
  next one, today included; "next Tuesday" and "before Tuesday" on a Tuesday
  mean the week after. No date when two different days are named, after
  "every"/"each", for plural day names, "daily"/"weekly", a day gone by
  ("from/since/last/past Monday"), a possessive ("Tuesday's email"), a name
  ("Black Friday", "Thursday Murder Club", "Monday.com"), or when it lands
  before today. Months and numeric dates are left to the AI.
- *The same-day rule.* Something set aside today (left out of today's
  picks, a locked-day dump, "Not today", swapped out) isn't for today just
  because its words say "today" or the AI read today's weekday: a date equal
  to the day it's saved is dropped, AI or not.
- *Captures (for 1.6 E).* `parkTasks(texts, dates?, refDays?)` (store) /
  `parkTasks(state, texts, now, dates, { today, refDays })` (task-extras):
  `refDays[i]` is the day item `i` was written (yyyy-MM-dd). Its words are
  read against that day, the same-day rule doesn't apply (it was captured for
  that day), a date already past when it's imported is dropped, except a
  deadline missed in the last week, which comes in as Overdue.
- *Carried through Today.* A saved item added to Today (Add, Swap in, or
  from last night's draft) keeps its date on the task (`Task.when`, also on
  the day's history record, so a carried-over task keeps it through the
  rollover). "Not today" or being swapped out saves it again with that date
  as it is (`carried: true`; never re-read from the words): a deadline that
  has passed stays Overdue, and a deadline set aside on its own day keeps
  its date; only a plan set aside on its own day loses it. Editing a task's
  words drops its date (like its steps) and reads one from the new words.
- Dates are read against the store's day (`lastOpenedDate` / the action's
  `today`), not the clock's, so flying across a date line doesn't shift them.

**Showing it.** Saved for later shows a quiet line under a dated item:
"Today", "Tomorrow", "Tue" within the week, else "Mar 1" ("Mar 1, 2027" in
another year); a deadline reads "By Tue". Once the day has passed, a plan
(`on`) shows no date and simply drops back into the list; a deadline (`by`)
reads "Overdue" and leads for 7 days (`OVERDUE_DAYS`), also when the app
wasn't opened on the day, then its label goes. **Tapping the date** opens
"Change day" (iOS: a menu; Android: choices under the item): Today,
Tomorrow, the next five weekdays, or **No date** (`setParkedWhen`, store
action `setParkedWhen`); a deadline stays a deadline.

**Resurfacing.** Never adds anything to today by itself; the three-task
limit and the lock are untouched. Plans read "For today" / "For tomorrow" /
"For Tuesday"; only deadlines say "Due …" (or "Overdue").

- *Ideas (everyone).* Saved items for today (and deadlines missed in the
  last week) lead the sheet under a small "For today" heading, above routines
  and suggestions, with 1.5 C's muted reason line; the rest stay under
  "Saved for later". Under the heading an item shows its label ("Today",
  "By today", "Overdue"), not the reason again. The saved-only sheet lists
  them first, with the reason line. A locked or
  full day keeps them greyed out as before (Swap in still works on a full
  unlocked day). Its hint no longer says "Your day is full" when it isn't.
- *Today (everyone).* The "Saved for later (N)" link also shows when
  something leads, even with room: "Saved for later (3) · 1 for today".
- *Evening draft (Plus, like the coach's draft).* At the close, saved items
  for tomorrow are put first in tomorrow's draft (`withDueTomorrow`), still
  at most three (the AI's later picks make room; leftovers are on the
  rollover card as before). Done on the phone after the reply, so it works
  with the old Worker and with the on-device fallback; nothing new is sent to
  the evening route. The because line only speaks for what's in the draft:
  the coach's stays when all its picks made it, otherwise "Something you
  saved is for tomorrow." / "A few things you saved are for tomorrow.".
  "Try again" is judged on the coach's answer before saved items are added,
  so a failed AI close still offers it (under the draft: "The coach couldn't
  add to it."). `evening_closed` counts what the coach (or the on-device
  close) drafted, not the saved items added. Free users' on-device close is
  unchanged.
- *Morning card.* A drafted task that is a saved item for today shows "For
  today" ("Due today" for a deadline) under it (`draftDueReasons`); using it
  takes it out of Saved for later as before.

**Not done.** No "Plus also reads dates like 'March 1'" hint: it would be
one more line in a calm sheet, for little.

**Privacy and analytics.** No new analytics (none of the new paths send an
event). The only new thing sent is the local date with Plus brain dumps;
the privacy policy says so: "With Plus, your device's current date is sent
with the brain dump … Nothing else new is sent." (republish the gist).

**Compatibility.** New app ↔ old Worker: `today` is ignored, no `when` comes
back, dates come from the device only. Old app ↔ new Worker: no `today`, so
the prompt says "when is null" and any `when` is dropped; old apps ignore the
field anyway. The new schema requires a nullable `when` on every item (strict
OpenAI schemas need every property required). **Deploy the Worker before the
app ships** (`cd server && npx wrangler@4 deploy`) so Plus dumps get dates
from day one.
