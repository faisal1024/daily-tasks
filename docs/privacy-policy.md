# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 2026-04-25_

Daily Tasks ("the app") is built around a simple promise: pick three tasks, lock your day, finish calmly. The app is designed to be local-first and to collect as little information as possible.

## What the app stores

- **Tasks and history.** Your daily tasks, completion records, streaks, and reminder settings are stored locally on your device using the operating system's app storage. They never leave your device.
- **Notification preferences.** Your reminder choices are stored alongside your other app data on the device.

## What the app does not collect

- The app does **not** require an account.
- The app does **not** sync your data to any server.
- The app does **not** sell or share personal data with third parties.
- The app does **not** include analytics or crash reporting.
- The app does **not** show ads or use advertising identifiers.

## Notifications

If you grant notification permission, the app schedules **local** reminders on your device to nudge you about the day's tasks. These reminders are generated entirely on the device and are not sent through any external service.

You can turn notifications off at any time from the app's Settings tab or from your device's system settings.

## Resetting your data

You can erase every task, history entry, and setting at any time using **Settings → Reset all data**. The action is immediate and cannot be undone.

Uninstalling the app from your device also removes all stored data.

## Children's privacy

The app does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.
