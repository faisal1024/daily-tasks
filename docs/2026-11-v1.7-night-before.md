# 1.7.0: The night before (plan, November 2026)

Status: plan, not built. Based on `main` at 2bbc43a (1.6.1 in App Review).

## 0. What the owner asked for, checked against the code

Reading confirmed, with two refinements:

- **"Brain dump that becomes a list"** already exists for *today*
  (`components/daily-tasks/brain-dump-sheet.tsx`) and for *Monday*
  (`sunday-reset-sheet.tsx`). Both **save everything**: closing the brain
  dump's review calls `onConfirm([], items)`, and the reset parks the whole
  dump. That is the opposite of "only if I select, we save it", so this needs
  its own sheet with its own rule: **nothing is written to app state until the
  user ticks it and taps Keep.**
- **"Adds it to the list"**: there is no tomorrow list. Tasks only live on
  today; tomorrow is `state.tomorrowDraft` (at most three strings, shown the
  next morning by `TomorrowDraftCard`) plus Saved for later (`parkedTasks`,
  whose 1.6 F soft dates bring items back on their day). So kept items become
  **saved items dated tomorrow**, and the first three also become
  **tomorrow's draft**. This is the Sunday reset's shape, so a skipped morning
  or a dismissed card loses nothing.

Not built: a separate "tomorrow" list, auto-adding to Today without the
morning tap, or any persistence of unticked lines.

## 1. The flow and its copy

### Entry points

1. **Today card, evenings** (`NightBeforeCard`, new, in the evening block of
   `app/(tabs)/index.tsx` under `CompletionReflection`/`EveningResult`).
   Shows when `nightTarget(today, hour)` is not null: from 6 PM
   (`NIGHT_BEFORE_HOUR = 18`), or earlier once the day is closed
   (`isDayClosed`), or before 4 AM (see edge cases). Not during first run.
   It does not need tasks on today or the evening check-in setting.
   - Title: **Tomorrow, out of your head**
   - Body: *Write down what's on your mind. Keep only what you tick.*
   - Button: **Plan tomorrow** · × (label "Not tonight") hides it for the night.
   - After a save: title **Tomorrow is ready**, body *3 on tomorrow's plan · 2
     saved for later*, link **Add more**.
2. **Optional reminder** (PR 2): Settings › Reminders › **Night-before
   reminder**, default off, 8:30 PM, time editable with the existing
   `TimePickerRow`. Title *Tomorrow*, body *Two minutes to get it out of your
   head?* Not sent once tonight's is saved or dismissed. Tapping it opens the
   sheet. Offered once, inline, on the sheet's done step: *Want a nudge at
   8:30 PM?* **Yes** / **Not now**.
3. Not in Settings as a feature switch, and not a second button inside the
   check-in: one card is enough.

On Sunday evenings, while `sundayResetCard(...)` returns a card, the night
card is hidden (the reset is the bigger version of the same thing). Once the
reset is done or dismissed it comes back.

### The sheet (`NightBeforeSheet`, three stages like the brain dump)

**Write.** Header **Tomorrow**. Hint: *Everything on your mind for tomorrow.
One per line is fine. Nothing is saved until you choose.* Same `TextInput` as
the brain dump (`MAX_BRAIN_DUMP_CHARS`, dictation works). Button: **Make it a
list**.

**Sorting.** *Turning it into a list…* (`announceLiveChange`).

**Review / select.** Header **Keep what matters**. Line: *Tick what to keep.
The rest is let go.* Rows are checkboxes, **none ticked** to start. The first
three ticked rows (list order) show a small **Tomorrow** label; later ticked
rows show **Saved for later**. Footer line when more than three are ticked:
*The first three go on tomorrow's plan. The rest are saved for later.*
- Links: **Tick all** / **Clear**, and **Edit** (back to Write, text intact).
- Primary button, by state: disabled **Tick what to keep** → **Keep 4** →
  with unticked rows **Keep 4, let go of 2**.
- Secondary: **Let it all go**.
- Closing with × on this stage asks: *Let these go? Nothing here has been
  saved.* **Keep choosing** / **Let go**. (Never a silent save, never a silent
  loss.)

**Done.** *Kept 4. Three will be waiting in the morning, marked "From last
night".* Button **Done**. Toast on Today: *Kept 4 for tomorrow.*

### Turning the dump into a list

- Everyone: `localBrainDump` (`lib/daily-tasks/ai-helpers.ts`).
- Plus: `requestBrainDump` with `forDay: target` and `today`, as `sortWeek`
  already does. No proxy change, no deploy.
- Free: the same shared pool of three free AI sorts (`claimFreeAiDump`,
  `freeAiDumpNotice`, `refundFreeAiDump`); after that the simple split with
  `FREE_LIMIT_NOTICE` and a quiet **See Plus** link. The AI's order puts its
  suggested three first, but still unticked.

### Where kept items go

One reducer action, `saveNightBefore`, does all of it:

1. Every ticked item is parked (`parkTasks`) with `when: { kind: "on", date:
   forDate }` unless its own words carry another date, and with the new
   `nightFor: forDate`. `fitSaved` caps at `MAX_PARKED_TASKS`; overflow uses
   `overflowLine`.
2. The first three ticked become/merge into `tomorrowDraft` (`from: "night"`).
3. Unticked lines are dropped. They were never in state.

If tomorrow already has a draft (`mergeNightDraft`, pure): picks the user made
themselves come first (an existing `from: "reset"` or `"night"` draft, then
tonight's ticks), then the coach's or the on-device close's lines fill what's
left, three at most. What doesn't fit the draft is still in Saved for later,
dated tomorrow, so 1.6 F brings it forward ("Saved for later (5) · 2 for
today").

### The "From last night" tag

Derived, never cleaned up: an item shows it while `nightFor === today`.

- Morning card rows: muted line *From last night* (the `reasons` prop of
  `TomorrowDraftCard`; it wins over "Due today").
- Today rows: where "Carried over" sits in `task-row.tsx`, moon icon, muted.
- Saved for later rows in `ideas-sheet.tsx`.
- On the night itself the saved rows just read "Tomorrow" (the existing
  `whenLabel`).
- Gone at the end of that day: the next day `nightFor < today`. A task carried
  over shows "Carried over" instead.

## 2. One evening story, one morning story

Evening block, top to bottom: check-in → coach's note → **one** tomorrow card.

- `setEveningClose` today keeps a `from: "reset"` draft. Change it to merge
  through `mergeNightDraft`, so a close after a night dump adds the coach's
  lines to free slots instead of replacing the user's.
- `EveningResult` stops listing the draft when `draft.from === "night"` (the
  night card's "Tomorrow is ready" state lists it); it keeps the note. No two
  lists of the same three.
- The free evening teaser (`showsEveningTeaser`) already hides when a draft
  exists (`hasDraft`). Correct: someone who just planned tomorrow shouldn't be
  shown a teaser of it.
- Morning: the existing `TomorrowDraftCard`, no second card. `because` is
  stored as *"You wrote these down tonight."*; `morningPerspective` already
  turns "tonight" into "last night".
- Rollover: unchanged. `pendingRollover` and the draft card already coexist
  (`rolloverOnTop`, `settlePendingRollover`). Carried tasks take slots, the
  card says "Room for one more today. Pick which.", and unticked night items
  stay in Saved with their tag. `applyTomorrowDraft` already unparks what
  lands on Today.

## 3. Free vs Plus

| | Free | Plus |
|---|---|---|
| Card, dump, simple split, select, save, tag, morning card, reminder | Yes | Yes |
| AI list (rewrites, splits run-ons, orders by what matters, reads dates) | 3 shared free sorts | Unlimited |
| Coach fills free slots at the evening close | No (on-device close) | Yes |

Reasoning: the habit (a reason to open the app at night and again in the
morning) is the retention win, and it is the free core: dump, pick, no pile.
Gating it would cripple the thing that makes people stay. The conversion lever
is that a nightly dump spends the three free AI sorts in three nights instead
of three weeks, at a moment the difference between a raw split and a clean
list is obvious. New `PaywallSource` `"night_before"` (headline *Wake up to a
clear list*), `PlusFeature` stays `"brain_dump"`. No hard gate, no paywall on
entry; `offerAhaPaywall()` is not called from this flow at night.

## 4. Data model and store

`lib/daily-tasks/types.ts`
- `ParkedTask.nightFor?: string`, `Task.nightFor?: string`,
  `DayTaskRecord.nightFor?: string` (so `buildDayTaskRecords` /
  `restoreTasksFromRecord` in `rollover.ts` keep it through a hydrate repair,
  like `when`).
- `TomorrowDraft.from?: "reset" | "night"`, `TomorrowDraft.night?: string[]`
  (which of `tasks` came from the night dump).
- `AppState.nightBefore?: { reminder: boolean; reminderMinutes: number;
  doneFor: string | null; dismissedFor: string | null; reminderAsked: boolean }`
  read through `nightBeforeOf(state)` (same pattern as `sundayResetOf`).

Reducer (`store.tsx`): `saveNightBefore { texts, forDate, nightOf, at, today,
source, dates? }`, `updateNightBefore { patch }`. `addTasks`/`addTask` take an
optional `nights` map the way they take `whens`; `applyTomorrowDraft`,
`addParkedTask` and `swapParkedTask` pass `nightFor` along.

Persistence (`storage.ts`): all fields optional, so no migration. Extend
`normalizeTomorrowDraft` (accept `from: "night"`, cap `night`), the parked and
task normalizers (`nightFor` must pass `isDateKey`), add
`normalizeNightBefore`. "Reset all data" clears it.

Day boundaries (`nightTarget(today, hour)` → `{ forDate, nightOf } | null`):
- 6 PM to midnight: `forDate = today + 1`, `nightOf = today`.
- **After midnight, before 4 AM**: `forDate = today`, `nightOf = today - 1`.
  The card reads **Plan today**; the draft shows straight away. `parkTasks`
  drops a same-day "on" date for something set aside today, so pass
  `refDays: nightOf` to keep it.
- **11:58 PM**: the target is captured when the sheet opens (as the reset's
  `plan` state does) and sent in the action, so a save at 12:03 still plans
  the day that was "tomorrow". The `rollover` case only clears drafts with
  `forDate < today`, so it survives.
- **Time zones**: date keys are local and `storeDayFor` already holds the day
  on a one-day clock step back. Flying east past the target day clears the
  draft at rollover, but every kept item is in Saved for later, so nothing the
  user chose is lost.
- **Skipped morning**: draft cleared at the next rollover; items stay saved,
  their date has passed (no label, per `whenLabel`), tag gone.
- **App killed mid-flow**: typed text and the unsaved list are component
  state only. Lost by design (see open question 3).
- An item that matches an open task on today is skipped by `parkTasks`; it can
  still be in the draft, and the rollover card will offer it anyway. Counts in
  the done line come from what was really written.

## 6. Analytics (no task text, existing allowed props only)

One event, `night_before`:
- `step`: `card` | `write` | `review` | `done` | `reminder`
- `action`: `shown` | `start` | `sorted` | `saved` | `let_go` | `close` |
  `dismissed` | `add_more` | `plus` | `on` | `off`
- `source`: `today` | `notification` | `whats_new`
- `kind`: `ai` | `local` | `free_ai`; `count` = kept; `skipped` = let go.

Also `tomorrow_draft_used` gains `kind: "night" | "reset" | "evening"`, and
`plus_gate_hit`/`paywall_viewed` use source `night_before`.

It works if, four weeks after release: 15%+ of users active after 6 PM start
it at least once; 60%+ of starts reach `saved`; the morning card for a night
draft is used at least as often as the evening draft's; users with a night
save return the next day more than matched users without; and trial starts
from `night_before` are not zero. Keep rate (`count / (count + skipped)`)
near 100% means the select step is noise; near 0% means the list is bad.

## 9. Open questions (defaults in bold; the build uses the default)

1. Ticks start empty or pre-ticked? **Empty**, with "Tick all". It's the
   literal reading of "only if I select".
2. Should kept items land on Today automatically in the morning? **No, one
   tap on the morning card**, like every other draft; it keeps rollover sane.
3. Keep a half-typed dump if the app is killed? **No** (nothing saved without
   a choice). Cheap to add later as a local, same-night scratch.
4. Undo for "let go"? **No undo; a confirm on × and counts on the button**
   instead.
5. Reminder default? **Off, asked once after the first save**, since the
   schedule is already about ten a day.
6. Tag wording? **"From last night"**.
7. Before 6 PM entry? **None** in 1.7 (the brain dump covers daytime).
8. More than three kept: choose by tick order or list order? **List order**,
   simpler to explain and to hear.
