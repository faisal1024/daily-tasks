# 1.7.0: The night before (plan, November 2026)

Status: built on `feature/night-before` (with the owner change below). Based on `main` at 2bbc43a (1.6.1 in App Review).

## Owner change (27 Nov 2026, during the build)

> "actually i want to keep the list till the morning. if its not saved for
> later or added, then it gets thrown out. i dont want to throw it out at night"

This replaces the night-time "keep only what you tick" model below (and open
questions 2 and 3). The build follows this section; where the rest of the doc
disagrees, this wins:

1. **At night nothing is thrown out and nothing is filed.** What's typed and
   the list made from it, ticked or not, are held in their own persisted
   state, `state.nightList`, until the morning. Not in Saved for later, not in
   `tomorrowDraft`. Ticking a row at night only marks it "first". Closing the
   sheet keeps everything (no "Let these go?" confirm); a half-typed dump is
   saved as it's typed, so it survives the app being killed.
2. **The morning decides.** On the day the list is for, Today shows one card,
   *Last night's list* (in the morning card's place; the evening draft card
   follows once it's done). Rows ticked "first" lead; every row says *From
   last night*. Per row: **Add** (to Today, with Today's room and lock rules)
   or **Save** (to Saved for later, tagged `nightFor`). **Let the rest go**
   ends it.
3. **What isn't added or saved is let go**: when the user taps Let the rest
   go, or at the rollover once that day has ended. Leftovers are never moved
   into Saved for later.
4. The tag is unchanged: "From last night" on what was added or saved, that
   day only (`nightFor === today`).
5. Reopening the sheet the same evening brings back the held list (editable:
   Edit goes back to the words, and making the list again keeps the ticks).
   After midnight (before 4 AM) the dump plans the day that has begun and the
   list is on Today straight away. A list still unresolved on its own day is
   replaced if a new night dump for the next day is started that evening (its
   day was ending anyway).

## 0. What the owner asked for, checked against the code

- **"Brain dump that becomes a list"** already exists for *today*
  (`components/daily-tasks/brain-dump-sheet.tsx`) and for *Monday*
  (`sunday-reset-sheet.tsx`). Both file everything at once. The night before
  files nothing at night (see the owner change above): it holds the list.
- **"Adds it to the list"**: there is no tomorrow list. Tasks only live on
  today, so the held list waits for the morning, where each row is added to
  Today or saved for later.

Not built: a separate "tomorrow" task list, adding to Today without a tap in
the morning, or moving leftovers into Saved for later.

## 1. The flow and its copy

### Entry points

1. **Today card, evenings** (`NightBeforeCard`, in the evening block of
   `app/(tabs)/index.tsx` under `CompletionReflection`/`EveningResult`).
   Shows when `nightTarget(today, hour, dayClosed)` is not null: from 6 PM
   (`NIGHT_BEFORE_HOUR = 18`), earlier once the day is closed, or before
   4 AM. Not during first run, not while the Sunday reset's card is up.
   - Title: **Tomorrow, out of your head**
   - Body: *Write down what's on your mind. It waits till morning.*
   - Button: **Plan tomorrow** (after midnight **Plan today**) · × (label
     "Not tonight") hides it for the night.
   - Once something is held: title **Saved for the morning**, body *6 items ·
     2 first* (or *Half-written. Open the list to finish it.*), button **Open
     list**, × "Hide".
2. **Optional reminder**: Settings › Reminders › **Night-before reminder**,
   default off, 8:30 PM, time editable with `TimePickerRow`. Title
   *Tomorrow*, body *Two minutes to get it out of your head?* Not sent once
   tonight's list is kept or the card was closed; skipped on a Sunday whose
   reset notification is planned. Tapping it opens the sheet. Offered once on
   the sheet's done step: *Want a nudge at 8:30 PM?* **Yes** / **Not now**.

### The sheet (`NightBeforeSheet`)

**Write.** Header **Tomorrow**. Hint: *Everything on your mind for tomorrow.
One per line is fine. It all waits till morning.* Button: **Make it a list**.
The words are held as they're typed.

**Sorting.** *Turning it into a list…*

**List.** Header **Your list for the morning**. Line: *Tick the ones to do
first. Nothing is thrown out tonight: in the morning you choose what to add
or save.* Rows are checkboxes, none ticked; a ticked row shows **First**.
Links: **Clear ticks** (when any), **Edit**. Button: **Keep till morning**.

**Done.** *Kept till morning: 6 items, 2 marked first. Then add what you want
to Today, or save it for later.* Button **Done**. Toast on Today: *Your list
is waiting for the morning.*

### The morning card (`NightMorningCard`)

Title **Last night's list**; *Add what you want to Today, or save it for
later. The rest is let go when you're done.* Each row: the words, *From last
night* (· *First*), **Add** and **Save**. With no room: *Today's three are
set. Save it for later instead.* (or *Today is set.…*); with Saved for later
full: *Saved for later is full.* Bottom: **Let the rest go**. The card goes
when the last row is added or saved.

### Turning the dump into a list

- Everyone: `localBrainDump`. Plus: `requestBrainDump` with `forDay` and
  `today` (no proxy change). Free: the shared three free AI sorts, then the
  simple split with `FREE_LIMIT_NOTICE` and a quiet **See Plus**.
- A dump typed but never made into a list is split on the device in the
  morning (`withTextSplit`), so nothing written is lost.

## 2. One evening story, one morning story

- Evening: check-in → coach's note → **one** night card. The evening close's
  draft is untouched by the night list.
- Morning: last night's list takes the morning card's place; the evening
  draft card follows once the list is done (one card at a time).
- Rollover: the list survives the night's rollover into its day and is let
  go at the next one.

## 3. Free vs Plus

| | Free | Plus |
|---|---|---|
| Card, dump, simple split, held list, morning card, tag, reminder | Yes | Yes |
| AI list (rewrites, splits run-ons, orders by what matters, reads dates) | 3 shared free sorts | Unlimited |

Reasoning: the habit (a reason to open the app at night and again in the
morning) is the retention win, and it is the free core: dump, pick, no pile.
Gating it would cripple the thing that makes people stay. The conversion lever
is that a nightly dump spends the three free AI sorts in three nights instead
of three weeks, at a moment the difference between a raw split and a clean
list is obvious. New `PaywallSource` `"night_before"` (headline *Wake up to a
clear list*), `PlusFeature` stays `"brain_dump"`. No hard gate, no paywall on
entry; `offerAhaPaywall()` is not called from this flow at night.

## 4. Data model and store

`lib/daily-tasks/types.ts`
- `Task.nightFor?`, `ParkedTask.nightFor?`, `DayTaskRecord.nightFor?` (kept
  through rollover records like `when`).
- `NightList { forDay, nightOf, text, items: { id, text, picked }[], source,
  dates?, createdAt }`, `AppState.nightList?: NightList | null`.
- `AppState.nightBefore?: { reminder, reminderMinutes, doneFor, dismissedFor,
  reminderAsked }` read through `nightBeforeOf(state)`.

Reducer (`store.tsx`): `setNightList { list }`, `updateNightBefore { patch }`,
`takeNightItem { id, how: "add" | "save", today, at }`, `finishNightList`.
`rollover` drops a list whose day has passed. `addTask`/`addTasks` take
`nightFor`/`nights`; `applyTomorrowDraft`, `addParkedTask` and
`swapParkedTask` pass it along.

Persistence (`storage.ts`): all optional, no migration. `normalizeNightList`
(rows cleaned like tasks, a bad row dropped, never the list; a bad list read
as null, never the save), `normalizeNightBefore`, `nightFor` must be a real
day. "Reset all data" clears both.

## 9. Open questions (defaults in bold; the build uses the default)

1. Ticks start empty or pre-ticked? **Empty**. Ticks only mark "first".
2. Superseded by the owner change: nothing lands anywhere until a tap per row
   in the morning.
3. Superseded: a half-typed dump is kept (saved as it's typed).
4. Undo for "let go"? **No undo**; at night nothing is let go at all, and in
   the morning the button says what it does.
5. Reminder default? **Off, asked once after the first Keep till morning**, since the
   schedule is already about ten a day.
6. Tag wording? **"From last night"**.
7. Before 6 PM entry? **None** in 1.7 (the brain dump covers daytime).
8. Order in the morning? **Ticked-first rows lead, each group in list
   order.**
